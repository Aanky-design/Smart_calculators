# PrepaySmart — Home Loan EMI, Step-Up & SIP Calculator
An interactive home loan payoff and wealth-building calculator built to help homeowners reduce their loan tenure, save on interest, and plan parallel investments.
## Features
- **Extra Annual EMIs:** See how paying 1 to 4 extra EMIs each year cuts down your total interest and loan duration, with flexible start years and duration windows.
- **Annual EMI Step-Up:** Model yearly EMI increases based on expected annual salary hikes, complete with custom start/duration windows and peak EMI tracking.
- **Parallel SIP Wealth Builder:** Calculate long-term wealth accumulation by investing a percentage of your monthly EMI into a step-up SIP alongside your home loan.
- **Visual Charts & Year-by-Year Schedule:** Compare your standard loan balance against your accelerated payoff and growing SIP corpus, view a complete annual amortization table, and export the schedule to CSV.
